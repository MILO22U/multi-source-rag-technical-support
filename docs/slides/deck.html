7Two results that went against me

−0.026
Hybrid fusion lost to BM25 alone. Synthetic passages share the question's vocabulary — BM25's best case — and LSA over 54 chunks is a thin latent space. Re-measure with real embeddings before generalising.
0.730
vs 0.718
Uniform chunking out-scored per-source on nDCG — but recall is structurally inflated by chunk size, and it lost a third of its conflict detection (0.857 → 0.571).
±0.19
95% CI on nDCG@10 with n=14. So I claim only the two effects bigger than the interval: reranking and score blending. Everything else is reported, not claimed.
A system optimising retrieval scores alone would pick the uniform splitter — and would then be unable to detect a third of its conflicts.
